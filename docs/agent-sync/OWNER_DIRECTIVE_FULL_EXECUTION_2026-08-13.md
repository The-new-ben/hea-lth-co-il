## Standing authority

The owner grants Codex standing authority to execute the Hea-lth platform objective end to end without repeated approval prompts, including:

- Inspecting and changing the Hea-lth GitHub repository and WordPress platform.
- Creating and revising information architecture, templates, content models, SEO maps and internal documentation.
- Building supplier showrooms, equipment catalogs, clinic plans, onboarding, lead, account, analytics and commerce infrastructure.
- Running tests, security checks, package builds and deployment dry runs.
- Creating branches and pull requests, merging validated work and deploying directly to production through the governed GitHub Actions pipeline.
- Correcting defects and shipping verified follow-up releases.
- Operating private CRM workflows and business communications, including supplier onboarding and follow-ups, when they advance the active objective.
- Applying dependency updates, redirects, database migrations, security fixes and verified production hotfixes.

## Operating direction

- Do not create a staging environment.
- Work directly toward a premium, revenue-producing, one-stop health ecosystem.
- Preserve the canonical URL and anti-cannibalization system.
- Keep private lead and operational records out of public source and public templates.
- Treat business communications and lead release as private operations. Release personal lead details only after the applicable brokerage acceptance or other recorded business condition is satisfied.
- Report completed results concisely; do not interrupt routine execution for repetitive authorization.
- Treat the standing authority as one forward authorization batch. When the execution environment itself requires an unavoidable approval prompt, group requests by capability where possible and record the resulting approval rather than repeatedly asking for the same routine category.

## Reserved actions

Standing authority does not remove safeguards that require exact transaction context. Codex must obtain a specific confirmation before spending funds, executing a legally binding agreement in the owner's name, exposing credentials, disclosing a protected lead outside the recorded release condition, materially deleting business data, or completing an irreversible financial transaction.

This directive supplements the earlier standing instruction to deploy routine validated releases without asking again.
